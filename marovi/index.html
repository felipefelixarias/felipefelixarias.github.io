Marovi AI: Learn AI and Machine Learning in Three Languages

A place to learn AI and machine learning in English, Spanish and Chinese: encyclopedia-style articles, summaries of landmark research papers, a concept glossary and a knowledge graph, with courses, practice problems and one ordered study plan. Why Marovi

Research shouldn't have a language barrier.

Most AI research, and most good explanations of it, are written in English. Marovi publishes every article, paper summary and course in English, Spanish and Chinese, connects them through a shared glossary and knowledge graph, and orders them into a study plan, so a reader can go from a paper to the concepts behind it without leaving their language. Readers can suggest corrections on any page.

Architecture

Full-stack, provider-agnostic, evaluation-driven.

  • Provider-agnostic API: routes across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and DeepL with unified interface, request batching, and response caching.
  • Agentic content and translation pipelines: glossary enforcement, structured output (Pydantic schemas), and multi-pass quality checks.
  • Reader correction loops: suggested edits, review, and propagation to the Spanish and Chinese versions.
  • Evaluation infrastructure: benchmark frameworks (FLORES, XL-SUM) and LLM judge systems for automated quality assessment.
  • Deployment: AWS with MediaWiki frontend, Python orchestration, and cost-optimized serving.

Recognition
