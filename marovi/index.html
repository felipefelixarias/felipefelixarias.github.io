Why Marovi

Research shouldn't have a language barrier.

Most of the world's research is published in English. Marovi makes it accessible to Spanish, Portuguese, French, and other language communities through high-quality, domain-aware translation with human-in-the-loop corrections.

Architecture

Full-stack, provider-agnostic, evaluation-driven.

  • Provider-agnostic API: routes across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and DeepL with unified interface, request batching, and response caching.
  • Agentic translation pipelines: glossary enforcement, structured output (Pydantic schemas), and multi-pass quality checks.
  • Crowdsourced correction loops: user edits, reviewer votes, and canonical version tracking across 9+ languages.
  • Evaluation infrastructure: benchmark frameworks (FLORES, XL-SUM) and LLM judge systems for automated quality assessment.
  • Deployment: AWS with MediaWiki frontend, Python orchestration, and cost-optimized serving.
