Urban Risk · Computer Vision

Video as a Sensor for Urban Risk

We combine weak supervision with multi-signal detection to surface risky roadway behavior when labeled data is scarce.

Felipe Felix Arias Daniel Carmody Richard Sowers Jayati Singh Kevin So

University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign

Overview

Risk-aware behavior analysis.

We identify scenes and compound objects (e.g., cyclist = person + bicycle) and track behavior over time to estimate risk in urban settings. The pipeline blends vision signals, heuristics, and weak supervision to generate labels at scale.

Tracking Cyclists

Preserving identity through occlusion.

Standard detectors drop cyclists when person or bicycle leaves the frame. We infer missing detections using temporal context to improve continuity.

Baseline detector.

Enhanced detections with temporal inference.

Risk Scoring

Weak supervision for rare events.

We generate probabilistic risk labels from pose, motion, and contextual heuristics, then use matrix completion to weight noisy signals.
